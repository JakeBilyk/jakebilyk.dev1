Projects: Professional and Personal

3D Printing

I started on a Creality Ender-3 Pro alongside Fusion360 back in 2021. I have created some useful inventions for around the house and in the laboratory. Then, I had the honor to design and print for hundreds of hours on a Bambu X1 Carbon at my previous job. I am a big fan of open-source equipment but I have to admit that it was a pleasure to be able to walk away after starting a print knowing success was near guaranteed. Now I run an Ender-6 at home and design in SolidWorks.

Programming

I believe all formal STEM Education should have a stronger focus on programming. My journey past Excel began with making small adjustments to MATLAB control scripts in 2018 and then writing scripts in MATLAB and Python for my Materials Engineering coursework and personal research. I have compiled my own Marlin firmware for my 3D printer and have written a few small programs in C and Python. Now, I definitely use AI services to speed up my work but I'm grateful to have had the opportunity to code some solid projects before the birth of consumer AI. My literacy in programming allowed me to implempent some serious IoT full-stack projects at NELHA that reduced labor and permitted GMP alerts / tracking. Go see my GitHub for more and future contributions!

DIY Auto

With help from friends I have cared for all my vehicles during their lifetimes. I can only hope my first car (Chelsea) is still running great somewhere in California after intermediate repairs such as a new thermostat housing, fresh calipers, and throttle body. I also brought an old 1996 convertible (The Hot Tamale) back to a legal functioning life by replacing the valve cover gaskets, spark plugs, rear drums, front brakes, and faulty electrical. Someone is still enjoying this during the Minnesota summers. Now I drive a beastly little Ford Focus (Lil Blue) approaching a quarter million miles here in Hawaii. I also ride motorcycle, a Kawasaki KLX 300 named Rambi.
